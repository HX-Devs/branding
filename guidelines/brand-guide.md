# Helix — Brand guide

## Purpose

**Crea con propósito.**

**Mission:** Damos forma a ideas que convierten lo complejo en nuevas posibilidades.

Helix is a software company in Mexico building its own products. Healthcare and insurance are the starting point; the identity also accommodates games and future categories. The intent is to create software that helps people and has a positive effect on their lives.

## Personality and voice

Calm, curious, precise, and approachable. Use generous space, restrained colors, clear hierarchy, and direct language. Explain what a product does before describing its technology. Avoid unsupported claims about clinical outcomes, security, compliance, or business results.

Use Spanish for the Mexico-facing company presence and English where the audience calls for it. Write Helix in running text; use HELIX in the wordmark.

- **Tagline:** Crea con propósito.
- **Mission:** Damos forma a ideas que convierten lo complejo en nuevas posibilidades.
- **Example:** “Consulta el estado de tu solicitud y revisa qué sigue.”

## Approved logo

The primary symbol is the intertwined HX with a continuous diagonal cut separating the H crossbar from the central stroke. The cut is transparent and must remain open.

Use the approved [horizontal logos](../logos/horizontal/) and [standalone symbols](../logos/symbols/). The primary horizontal treatment uses Clay for HX and Ink for HELIX. Ink and Ivory versions provide monochrome alternatives.

### Height and construction

HX and HELIX must have the same **visible height**, aligned at both the top and bottom. Include strokes when measuring; do not align only the SVG file boxes.

In the horizontal masters, both have visible bounds from y=29 to y=135, for a height of 106 units. The 100-unit symbol is scaled by 1.06; the 100-unit wordmark includes its 6-unit stroke. The animation uses a shared 100-unit visible cap height throughout its transition.

The symbol has reinforced stems and diagonals. Preserve its geometry and negative space. Do not stretch, rotate, close the cuts, or add shadows, outlines, or gradients.

### Clear space and size

Leave at least one quarter of the symbol width clear on each side. Start with a minimum standalone symbol height of 24 px on screens and inspect the diagonal cuts at the actual rendered size. Favicon sizes may require a separately reviewed optical adjustment.

## Color

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory | `#F5F2EB` | Main background |
| Ink | `#292C29` | Primary text and monochrome symbol |
| Clay | `#A45C47` | Main brand accent and primary actions |
| Sage | `#7E8B76` | Decorative secondary accent |
| Mist | `#E4E7DE` | Secondary surfaces |

Favor neutral space over large accent areas. Ink on Ivory is the default reading combination. Ivory on Clay is the intended filled-action treatment. Check contrast for actual text sizes and interface states; do not use Sage as small text on Ivory. Reserve separate semantic colors for errors, warnings, and success, and do not communicate status through color alone.

The landing footer uses near-black `#111111` with Ivory text and symbol.

## Typography

Use a warm editorial serif for short marketing headlines and a clear sans serif for body copy and interfaces. Current examples use Georgia and Arial as available stand-ins; final editorial font selection and licensing remain open. The logo is vector artwork and has no font dependency.

Keep healthcare, insurance, and data-heavy interfaces predominantly sans serif. Favor sentence case and comfortable line spacing.

## Brand architecture

Helix is the parent company. Products can have their own names and palettes, supported by an “Un producto de Helix” endorsement. Games can have more expressive identities with a quiet Helix credit. Avoid defining the corporate identity through medical or insurance imagery alone.

## Imagery and motion

Use purposeful diagrams, product screenshots, and restrained illustrations. Avoid generic stock imagery as the main expression of the corporate brand.

The approved motion reduces HELIX into HX through horizontal compression, an early fade on contraction, a late reveal on expansion, and a damped bounce. Keep the height constant and respect reduced-motion preferences. See [motion documentation](../animation/README.md).

On the landing, the navbar begins with HELIX and contracts into HX when the visitor scrolls; the hero displays a static HX symbol.
